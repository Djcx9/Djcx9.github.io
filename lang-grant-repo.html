Core Funding Streams Framework

Why foundational communication tiers require alternate funding mechanisms outside federal immigrant settlement caps.

The Settlement vs. Workplace Disconnect

Standard immigration language streams (like LINC) prioritize public settlement tracks and often target higher benchmarks (CLB 4+) needed for permanent residency or citizenship milestones. Frontline workers starting at pre-literacy or CLB 1–2 face severe communication gaps on day one of employment that slow down safety and operational throughput.

Employer Co-Investment Leverage

Provincial job grant frameworks bridge this divide by covering up to two-thirds (66%+) of third-party trainer fees, custom materials, and software expenses for targeted essential skills development.
