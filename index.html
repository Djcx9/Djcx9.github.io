Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost?

The audit and the debrief are free. The pilot is scoped and priced at the debrief, once we know the bottleneck. There is no price before we know the work, and no retainer.

What do I get from the audit?

A scorecard by email, shortly after you finish. It names your biggest time thief and the one bottleneck to fix first.

What happens in the debrief?

Thirty minutes. We walk through your bottleneck, who owns it, and how we would measure the fix. You leave with a Queue Map, an Hours Baseline, a Hand-Off Plan, and a Go/No-Go Note, whether or not we work together.

What if there is no clear bottleneck?

Then there is no pilot. If the bottleneck, the owner, and the measure cannot be validated, we do not sell you one. That is the rule.

How long does a pilot take?

Weeks, set at the debrief. It depends on the bottleneck. Steps one to three take days, not weeks.

Do I need to learn a new system?

No. The work fixes one workflow you already have, and we train your team so it sticks.

What do you do with my information?

Your email is used only to send your Time-Back Scorecard, one follow-up reminder about your free debrief, and, if you ask, to discuss results. Your audit answers are used to build your scorecard and your pack, and nothing else.
